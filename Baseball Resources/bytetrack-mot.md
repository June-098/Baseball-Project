## What is ByteTrack MOT?
- It is a Multi-object tracking algorithm that allows you to track multiple objects in your scene. It can be used with an object detector of your choice and for each object tracked, it will have a bounding box and and ID. What truly sets ByteTrack apart from others is its use of not only the standard high threshold, but also a low threshold.
