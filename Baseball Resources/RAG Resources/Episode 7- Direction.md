## Money Gap = Direction

## What is the money gap?

The money gap is once I swing, I want to be able to finish and there better be no rollover. There should be a gap right here. Supination on my top hand form. Top hand Palm up, Bottom hand palm down. This is where we see the money gap or textbook-hitting form when we make the contact.

## Direction Drill
### 1. Top-hand drill

Goal: created our role in getting it to play a little early with our top hand vis-a-vis just like we're in infield turning two.

Intention: This is the feel how are top hand is moving when we are batting. One of the thing we want to feel is creating direction where we want to put the ball in desired direction with our top hand. We are going to work on this drill to create the roll and make a right path. 


Action: Because hitting baseball is similar to throwing a baseball, imagine Just like how third baseman is turning to the second base.
1. Imagine you are a third baseman
2. Turning two
3. Load your scapula
4. Switch to throw motion
5. Snap


### Bottom-Hand Drill
Goal: we're creating body movements and letting our back shoulder do all the work and it gives us a perfect direction on where we want to hit the ball.

The purpose: This drill is I want to stay connected.  I want to create body movements to where I want to hit the baseball and where I swing my back.

Instruction: Using the bottom hand, I'm going to put my fingers with the baseball facing away from me, connected to my back shoulder and I'm going to create direction to wherever I want to go. So I want to go to left center. I'm just going to rotate to left center, release and the ball goes to left center. If I want to go to center field, I'm just going to land, take my back shoulder to center field, I'm connected and there's the ball on center field.
