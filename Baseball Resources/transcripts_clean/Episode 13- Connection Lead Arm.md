## What is Lead hand/arm?
When we're doing our lead hand, first of all, what is the lead hand?

The lead hand is the guide.

It's the steering wheel to the car.

You can own a Ferrari, but if that steering wheel is all over the place, that Ferrari's not gonna drive straight and fast.

So our lead hand is our guide and it's our steering wheel and it needs to drive straight.

For an example, a lot of guys mis lead the connection. I don't wanna be a pusher, I wanna be connected.

Guys will land, push their hands, then try to hit.

When in reality, I wanna stay connected to my back shoulder, my back shoulder does the roll, it needs to stay connected to my lead hand and then once I'm pretty much square to the picture, then I wanna release the back.
