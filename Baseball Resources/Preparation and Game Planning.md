**Preparation and Game Planning**

- **Graduating to Velocity:** Manny and Ortiz often used the first pitch of an at-bat to "graduate" themselves to the pitcher's velocity. By taking a pitch, they could see the ball's movement and determine where the pitcher felt comfortable attacking them, which is a reality that cannot be fully captured by watching TV.
- **Pre-Game Study:** Preparation involved watching videos of how a pitcher approached hitters both with nobody on base and in scoring position to build a mental map before stepping into the box
